\caption{PointProgrammerNet architecture. The shared encoder writes each observation into three equal-size additive FWP states, after which the historical point list can be discarded. For segmentation, the states are read at a query coordinate and combined with a direct coordinate skip. For classification, soft row summaries from the fine and broad states form a bounded residual around the middle-state summary. Neither head pools over decoded points.}
